## Overview

RealtimeCollab is a collaborative whiteboard designed for remote teams who need to brainstorm and visualize ideas together in real-time, regardless of their location.

## Key Features

- **Real-time Sync**: Sub-50ms latency for seamless collaboration
- **Infinite Canvas**: Unlimited space for your ideas
- **Rich Media**: Support for images, sticky notes, and drawings
- **Version History**: Travel back in time to see how ideas evolved

## Architecture

The application uses a CRDT-based approach for conflict resolution, ensuring that all participants see consistent state even with network partitions.
